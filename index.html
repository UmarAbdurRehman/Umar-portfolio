01 / Profile

Engineer First,
Commercial Second.

I am a mechanical engineer from the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), currently working as a Technical Sales Engineer with Bin Shehab Co. in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. My work sits exactly where engineering meets the customer: reading the application, specifying the right equipment or wear part, estimating it honestly, and managing the account until the solution is running on site.

That means I speak two languages fluently in the same meeting — the technical one with maintenance and operations teams about metallurgy, tolerances, fabrication and equipment life, and the commercial one with procurement about lead time, landed cost and total cost of ownership. Heavy industry does not buy brochures; it buys uptime.

My background spans business development, technical sales, industrial solutions, design & estimation, wear-parts manufacturing, and account & project management across water, oil & gas, drilling, mining, cement, asphalt, locomotive and metal recycling sectors. Earlier, at NUSTAG I worked on Shell Eco-marathon vehicle development and engine ECU tuning — the hands-on, dyno-and-data side of engineering that still shapes how I approach every specification today.

Happy to connect, engineer, manage and solve. Enthusiastic to travel, explore, collaborate and innovate.

04 / Engineering Projects

Selected work

Design work where the constraint was cost, not capability — proving that a properly engineered system can match commercial equipment at a fraction of the price.

Flagship Project

Complete Engine Dynamometer System
for Engines up to 200 cc

Dynamometers are critical testing equipment in the automotive industry — they are how engines and engine technology get tested and tuned for better mileage, lower fuel consumption and reduced emissions. Commercial systems, however, are priced far beyond the reach of most university labs and small workshops.

The purpose of this project was to design a complete engine dynamometer system for engines up to 200 cc that performs comparably to commercially available solutions at a significantly lower cost — covering the absorption unit, mounting and coupling arrangement, load control, and the instrumentation needed to capture torque, speed and derived power across the engine's operating range.
